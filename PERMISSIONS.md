# Asking permission for non-academic use

Lableit is released under the [Lableit Academic Research License](./LICENSE).
It is free to use, modify, and share **for academic purposes only**: teaching,
learning, and non-commercial research at universities, colleges, and non-profit
research institutes.

**Every other use needs written permission first.** This includes, for example:

- use inside, or for the benefit of, a for-profit company;
- using Lableit, or annotations or datasets made with it, in a product,
  service, paid deliverable, or client project;
- government or agency operational use (not research);
- running Lableit as a hosted or online service for people outside your own
  research group or course;
- selling, sublicensing, or redistributing Lableit as part of any non-academic
  offering.

If you are not sure whether your use counts as academic, **ask first**. We are
happy to help, and many requests can be approved quickly.

## How to ask

Email the copyright holder with:

1. your name, organization, and role;
2. what you want to use Lableit for, and whether any part of it is commercial;
3. roughly how many people or deployments are involved, and for how long.

## What permission does not cover

Permission from us covers **only Lableit's own code**. It does **not** give you
rights to the SAM3 model weights or the `sam3` software package, which are
governed by Meta's separate **SAM License**, or to any other third-party
dependency. You must comply with those licenses yourself. See
[`NOTICE`](./NOTICE) and [`THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.md`](./THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.md).
